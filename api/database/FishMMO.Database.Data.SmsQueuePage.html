Remarks

Shaped as EmailQueuePage is, for the same reasons: the counts and both oldest-row ages are over the whole table, and the age of the oldest unclaimed row is the alarm. A player who chose verify-by-SMS is as stuck by a dead SMS drain as an email player is by a dead mail drain, and just as silently.
