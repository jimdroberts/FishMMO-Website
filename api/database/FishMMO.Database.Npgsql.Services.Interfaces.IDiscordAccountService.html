The Discord bot's side of an account: delivering the one verification DM, and the account link.

public interface IDiscordAccountService

Remarks

Codes are issued by PersistDiscordVerifyCodeAsync(string, int, CancellationToken) and redeemed by PersistVerifiedByCodeAsync(string, int, CancellationToken), alongside email and SMS. This service only moves a delivery through claimed, sent or released; see DiscordVerification for why a claim is never reclaimed.
