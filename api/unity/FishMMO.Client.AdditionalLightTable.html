Remarks

On WebGPU URP's lighting cbuffers are plain globals (FishShadowVariants.hlsl: the pipelines must stay within the device's 10 dynamic uniform buffers), which put ~14 KB of arrays into each lit program's $Globals; Unity copies a program's whole $Globals on every pass application, and that copy was the largest engine cost left in the browser (measured 2026-11-03).

The table is not a second account of URP's light setup. A compute kernel (FishLightTableGather, in the profile's terrain-instancing compute, as the depth pyramid's kernels are) declares URP's arrays by URP's names and sizes, so its constants are filled from the very globals URP has just set, and copies them bit for bit. It runs after the shadow passes, when the last of them (the additional lights' shadow parameters and slices) has been set, and before anything is lit; every camera runs its own, so each camera's draws read its own lights.
