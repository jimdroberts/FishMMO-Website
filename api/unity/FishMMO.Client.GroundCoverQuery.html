What the ground's procedural cover offers the rest of the client (Phase 4 of the core and modules plan, 2026-11-18): the grass cover at a point, for footsteps, the wind's beds and ambient life's foraging, and whether the grass or the detail scatter is still being built, for the frame series and the perf probes. The Terrain module installs the sources; with no Terrain module there is no cover, nothing is building, and every reader gets bare ground.
