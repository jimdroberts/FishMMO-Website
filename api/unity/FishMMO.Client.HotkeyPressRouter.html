Remarks

The bar is decided once, at the press, and remembered until the key comes up. Without that, letting go of Ctrl before letting go of 1 would release the first bar's slot — which never started anything — and leave the second bar's charged ability held until its hold cap cancelled it; and pressing Ctrl while 1 is already down would move a press that is still in progress onto a different ability.
