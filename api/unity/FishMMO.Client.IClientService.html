FishMMO.Client.dll

A module's piece of the client that lives as long as the client does (Phase 4 of the core and modules plan, 2026-11-18): the weather's mirror of the scene server's weather. The client makes one of each registered service when it starts, ticks them every frame, clears them when the world is left or the scene server changes, and shuts them down with itself.
