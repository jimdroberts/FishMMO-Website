Remarks

The needle is painted (north half in --map-north, south half faint) and turned by its rotation, so it points exactly; the letter is placed at the tip rather than turned with it, so it reads at any angle. Styled by .map-compass in UIMap.uss. Draws over the map and takes no clicks.
