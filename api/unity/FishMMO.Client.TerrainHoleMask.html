A terrain's painted holes (GetHoles(int, int, int, int)) as the detail renderers read them: the blade grass (folded into its water line, GrassTerrain.Freeboard), the GPU detail scatter (a mask slice beside its heights; every mesh kind since the CPU chunk renderer went, 2026-10-27). Unity's own detail drawing is off on every terrain they drive, so nothing else keeps a plant out of a cave mouth: the detail layers under a hole still hold whatever the biome scatter painted before the hole was cut, and the 2 m density texels and scatter blocks reach past the hole's edge even where they do not.

Remarks

One cell per heightmap cell (UnityEngine.TerrainData.holesResolution = heightmap resolution − 1), cell (x, z) spanning heightmap samples x..x+1 and z..z+1; the terrain shader clips a whole texel (ClipHoles), so a point test of the cell under a root matches the drawn edge.

PlantClearMetres

How far past a hole's edge no plant roots, metres (2026-10-27, Moron 2: "the ground details don't overlap the hole itself"): the 2 m density texels and scatter blocks spread what the cells beside an opening hold up to its edge, and a blade, a bush or a scattered stone rooted on that edge leans or spreads out over the opening. The cut clears the detail cells round every cave opening wider still (PointOfInterestCaveOpeningClearance.DetailMargin); this is the plants' own margin at every hole, which also keeps them out from under a canyon wall's or a cloud island's rim of rock.

public const float PlantClearMetres = 1.5
