Remarks

The panel only moves these strings and numbers into its elements. Keeping the wording and the arithmetic here lets a test pin every queue, every reason, every estimate and every edge of the progress bar without mounting a panel or a network.

Remarks

A full world's ended wait says plainly that trying again soon keeps the place. Parameters

Returns

Remarks

The three waits look the same from outside and mean different things: the world being full, a zone starting up, and the player's own body still standing in a fight they disconnected from. Only the last is self-inflicted, and a player who is not told about it cannot understand why they wait while the world is visibly not busy.

Remarks

At the front of the line the bar is full, including a wait that started there.
