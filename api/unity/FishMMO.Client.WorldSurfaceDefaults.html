Remarks

Core (Phase 4 of the core and modules plan, 2026-11-17; the "core surface stack" decision). FishWeatherLit and FishVegetation, with their includes (Assets/FishMMO/Shaders/Surface), are core rendering: core content (the characters, the teleporter, the gathering nodes) is drawn with them. Their globals are core-owned, and the world modules only set them: the sky its ambient, the weather its wind, cover and snow line, the terrain its ground maps.

Unset globals read zero, and the surface shaders are written so that zero is the neutral world: no cloud, rain, wind, cover or season; every map off until its rect, info or switch says it is set; the distance blends and the vegetation fade off while their bands are empty. The one exception is the ambient light, which at zero is black: it is set here once before the first scene loads, and the sky overwrites it every frame when it is installed.
