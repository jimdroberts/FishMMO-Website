Remarks

DateTime.UtcNow is the wrong clock for a duration. It is the host's wall clock, and NTP or an operator is free to step it: forward, and everything timed against it ages at once (every instance within the step of its lifetime cap closes on the next pulse, with its players inside); backward, and nothing ages until the clock catches up again. A duration only needs a clock that never goes backwards and never jumps, which is what this is.

The value is seconds from an arbitrary origin, so it means nothing on its own and nothing outside this process. Only differences between two readings are meaningful. It is Stopwatch underneath, so it is safe to read from any thread, which UnityEngine.Time is not.

An instant that has to mean the same thing in another process, or survive a restart, is not a job for this clock. Take it from the database, which every process shares.
