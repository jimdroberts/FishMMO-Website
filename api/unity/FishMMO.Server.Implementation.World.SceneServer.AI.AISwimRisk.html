The decision an NPC makes when the way to its target is through deep water: scored, never scripted. The risk is how long it would be in the water against how good a swimmer it is, with its breath on top for one that swims under; its appetite for risk is its courage, scaled by how much it wants this target. In if the appetite covers the risk; otherwise it waits on the shore; and past its leash, it lets the target go.
