Remarks

A plain runtime object, owned by whatever founded it. A spawner whose pack is enabled (Enabled) founds one when its first NPC spawns and adds every NPC it spawns after that, with the role its entry names; a boss founds one for the adds it calls in. It used to be a scene MonoBehaviour with inspector-assigned members and its own Update, and nothing ever created one: NPC brains are added to pooled instances at runtime on the server, so there was never a brain in a scene for an inspector to point at, and Group was never set.

Membership is the living members. A brain leaves on death (SuspendForCorpse()), on despawn and pool reset (FishMMO.Server.Implementation.World.SceneServer.AI.AIController.ResetForPool()) and when it is destroyed with its scene. A pack whose last member leaves is Released: it stops ticking, refuses members, and its founder makes a new one for the next spawn. So a respawn rejoins its spawner's pack while any member of it still stands, and founds the next one after a wipe.

Ticked by the brain host, inside the AI contract. AIBrainHost calls Tick(float, int) on every network tick; the pack thinks only on AI ticks (the same whole divisor of the network tick the brains use), only while a member is in a tier that fights (TierEvaluates(AILodTier)), and every EVALUATE_INTERVAL seconds scaled by that member's LOD interval. A pack with nobody near it costs a tier read per member per AI tick and does nothing else.

angles List<float>

Remarks

Fitted to where the members already stand, never to the world axes. The slots used to be absolute angles from world +X, so a Flank's "front" was east whatever the enemy faced and a FocusFire cluster formed on the east side even when the pack had come from the west. Now:

  • Surround — an even ring, rotated to the angle that moves the members least (the circular mean of each member's bearing less its ring step), members kept in the order they stand around the enemy.
  • Kite — the same ring advanced by kiteAdvance, so the ring turns from wherever the members are rather than from a stored angle they have drifted away from.
  • FocusFire — a CLUSTER_SPREAD arc centred on the members' mean bearing: they converge from the side they are already on.
  • Flank — tanks at flankFront, everyone else across the rear half-circle in the order they stand; a single flanker directly behind. (It used to put a lone flanker at the side, 90° from the front.)

Pure, so every tactic's geometry can be pinned without a scene.
