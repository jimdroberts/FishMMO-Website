How an NPCGroup arranges the members fighting its focus. Each such member is given a bearing around the focus (PackSlotAngle), which OrbitState steers to on the pack's TacticOrbitRadius.

public enum PackTactic

Fields

Flank = 2

The tank holds the front and everyone else spreads across the rear half-circle; a lone flanker goes directly behind. The front is the tank's side, or, with no tank fighting the focus, the direction the enemy faces.

FocusFire = 3

Members close in from the side they are already on, in a tight arc. Combined with FocusTargeting for maximum single-target pressure.

Kite = 4

A Surround ring that keeps turning, at KiteRotationSpeed. Best for ranged/caster groups that want to avoid melee contact.

None = 0

No coordinated positioning — members act independently.

Surround = 1

Members spread evenly around the focus, the ring rotated to move them least. Best for mixed groups that want to prevent the enemy from fleeing.

Remarks

Bearings are fitted to where the members already stand (see AssignFormation(PackTactic, IReadOnlyList<NPCGroupRole>, IReadOnlyList<float>, float, float, List<int>, List<float>)), not measured from the world axes. A tactic shapes the orbit manoeuvre only: an archetype whose attacking state has no Orbit variety state never orbits, and its approach is spaced by the combat-slot ring as any attacker's is.
