Remarks

Driven by what the pet knows, not by its archetype. A pet's spells can come from its summon spell (AbilityOnSummonEvent), so a melee pet may well know a heal. The heals and buffs are found the way HealerAttackingState finds its heals — from the ability's own ECA actions (AIAbilityClassifier) — and a pet that knows none finds nothing to do here. Run in combat by BaseAttackingState before the attack, and out of combat by PetIdleState, so a pet patches its owner up after a fight as well as during one.

The order is fixed. The owner is looked after first, at any distance: an owner below the heal threshold is healed, and an owner missing a buff the pet can give is buffed, before anyone else is considered — the pet walks back to its owner to do it. Then the most wounded of the pet itself and its friends, then their buffs. AIPetSupportSettings tunes the threshold and the reach; it cannot reorder this.

A friend is someone the pet's owner may not harm and is on the same side as: the owner's party, guild and other pets, and anyone the owner is allied with (IsFriend(Pet, ICharacter)).

Static and single-threaded, like the healer's ally sweep: every call runs to completion inside one synchronous AI tick, which is what lets the filters below share one scratch recipient instead of allocating a closure per pick.
