Class WildlifePlanner

Namespace
FishMMO.Server.Implementation.World.SceneServer.Spawner
Assembly
FishMMO.Server.dll

Plans a world scene's wildlife spawners when it is cut: big overlapping areas over the open country, each with the species its biomes support, plus a group at each den point of interest and some cave mouths. The cut writes the plan into the scene as ObjectSpawners (WildlifeSpawnerCut), which the spawn-table bake carries to the server like any other. At spawn time the runtime keeps each species on its own ground and its herd together (SpawnerRuntime).

Plan(string, Rect, GroundSampler, IReadOnlyList<PointOfInterestRecord>, Func<float, float, bool>, IReadOnlyList<SceneCaveLifeSpot>, IReadOnlyList<Species>, WildlifeCatalogue)

The plan for one scene: big overlapping areas over the open country, each holding the species whose ground covers enough of it (weighted by how much, and by their density), then small single-species areas at dens and cave mouths. Dens come first so the cap never starves them. Pure and deterministic (seeded by the scene's name), so it is tested on its own; the cut writes it into the scene as ObjectSpawners.
