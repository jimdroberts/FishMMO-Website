Remarks

The only effect a PetAbilityTemplate accepts, and one no other ability does (IsCompatibleWith(AbilityTemplate)). Crafted onto a summon, or bundled into one through OnSummonEvents, it does two things on the server once the pet has spawned:

  • PetAbilities are taught to the pet, merged with the spell's own PetAbilities before the spawn (BuildPetAbilityIDs(Ability, List<int>)).
  • The inherited conditions and actions run with a PetEventData whose target is the new pet, so an action with no selector acts on the pet (a buff, a heal) and InitiatorTargetSelector reaches the summoner instead.

The pet keeps what it was taught until it is dismissed or summoned fresh: the abilities are saved with the pet, so a pet restored at login still has them, and a new summon rebuilds the list from the spell as it stands then.
