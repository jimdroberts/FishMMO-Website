Remarks

The only impact position that survives leaving the rewind scope. AbilityObject.ResolveSweptHits queries inside a rewind to the caster's view and then dispatches the results after it has closed — deliberately, so no damage or authored action ever runs against a displaced world. Point therefore describes a world that no longer exists by the time anything reads it, and comparing it against anything read from a live transform mixes the two. At 200 ms that is metres.

A LOCAL point has no such problem, because the body and everything defined relative to it were displaced together: the relationship is identical in the rewound world and the live one. That is what lets ShieldVolume be tested against a hit long after the scope has closed. Zero when the hit resolved to no body.
