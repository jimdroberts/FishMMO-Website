Remarks

This is the reward hook. Rewards themselves are not decided here — currency, items, titles and achievements are a later system's business — but everything they will need is on ArenaMatchResult, and two ways to react are wired now: this C# event, and the template-authored trigger lists (WinRewardTriggers, LossRewardTriggers, DrawRewardTriggers) the coordinator runs on each present character right after raising it. A reward system subscribes here or authors triggers; the coordinator does not change.

Raised on the scene server that hosted the match, on the main thread, after the result is decided and before the results screen is sent. Subscribers must not throw.
