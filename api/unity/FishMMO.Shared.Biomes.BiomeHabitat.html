The kinds of country a biome offers the ambient creatures (birds, bats, small animals, insects) that live on it, as its template names them (BiomeTemplate.Habitat). The same members, by the same bits, as the client's own AmbientHabitat, which a test keeps equal: the client reads the template's value as its own.

Remarks

Until 2026-10-16 the client read a biome's country from keywords in its name (AmbientGating.HabitatOf: "forest", "marsh", "coast"…), and called ground under another world's air alien as well. The biome art authoring now writes that reading onto every template from the spec table, so a biome's animals are its template's to decide. Stored by number: never renumber a member. Alien alone is never stored: it is read live from the template's air (BiomeHabitats.Live).
