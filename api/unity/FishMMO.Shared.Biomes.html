Namespace FishMMO.Shared.Biomes

Classes

BiomeAffinity

How strongly a race belongs in a biome. A race's affinities say where its settlements are named from, where spawners should favour it, and — through the biome's climate variants — how its places read under the current weather.

BiomeHabitatQuery

The habitat of the ground at a point (Phase 4 of the core and modules plan, 2026-11-18): what the spawners ask before they place a creature. The World Simulation module installs Source from the scene's biome map; with none installed, or no map in the scene, every point answers None and HasMap is false, so a spawner places its creatures anywhere, as it does in a scene with no biome map.

GroundMakeup

What a ground is made of, read from its layer's name: x living (grass, moss, heath), y lichen and coral, z mineral (rock, sand, soil, ash), as a terrain layer's makeup stores it. Core (Phase 4 of the core and modules plan, 2026-11-18) so the baked terrain arrays read it without the World Simulation module; BiomeLooks.GroundShareOf forwards here.

Enums

BiomeHabitat

The kinds of country a biome offers the ambient creatures (birds, bats, small animals, insects) that live on it, as its template names them (BiomeTemplate.Habitat). The same members, by the same bits, as the client's own AmbientHabitat, which a test keeps equal: the client reads the template's value as its own.

Delegates

BiomeHabitatQuery.HabitatSource

Answers whether the scene has a biome map covering the point, and the habitat there; installed by the module.
