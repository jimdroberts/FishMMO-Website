Remarks

Where. Placement in the Frame, Distance metres from the target's centre. The spot is found the way TeleportCasterAction finds a landing: a capsule of the caster's size is swept out from the target a step high, stops short of anything in the way, and is dropped onto the ground there. A side with no room (the target's back against a wall) gives way to the next side that has it (ResolveDestination(PhysicsScene, Vector3, float, Vector3, BlinkPlacement, float, float, float, int)), so the blink never lands inside a wall or inside the target. Above checks there is headroom over the target and falls back the same way when there is not. The caster is turned to face the target.

Server only, deliberately, like KnockbackHitAction's displacement. A hit is not run again when the owner replays a correction, so a predicted blink would be undone by every correction from before the hit and redone a round trip later: the caster would flicker between the two places. Moved by the server, the owner instead receives the new position in its next reconcile and is moved once, a round trip after the hit. A player moves through its motor (TeleportTo(Vector3, Quaternion)), an NPC through its brain (BlinkTo(Vector3, Quaternion), onto the NavMesh). Refused while rooted, incapacitated, dead or changing scene.

Effects play on every client at once, for the hit it saw: DepartFX where the caster is and ArriveFX where it will land, which on an observer is measured from the characters as it draws them.
