Remarks

Brighter than the walls' torches. The cut's mine and cave-camp torches are lit by SiteLightPool at about 1.6 with a three-beat flicker and a warm orange, and its buried lamps at 2.2, steady: marks along a way, not light to see it by. A carried torch uses the same flicker and very nearly the same colour (a shade redder, nearer a pitch flame's 2200 K), but at the wall torches' strength it lit the rock within an arm's length and left the rest black in the cave walkthrough render (2026-10-13: "Ensure we can see and use the torch or a stronger light source for visual clarity"). URP point lights fall off with the square of distance whatever their range, so the strength is what carries: the torch reads the floor and walls a room's width off, the lantern most of a hall's. Underground the sky's ambient falls to under a tenth (SkySystem's darkness), so these are most of what lights a cave.

Ahead, not at the hand. Parented to what the character's model is drawn under (the smoothed graphical object, HolderFor(ICharacter)), not to a hand bone: an arm swinging through a walk would swing every shadow in the cave with it, and the race model under the mesh root is destroyed and rebuilt whenever it reloads. It sits forward of the chest at shoulder height, so the body's own shadow falls behind it (away from a camera that follows from behind), and the front of the character, not the back the camera sees, takes the light's hot near field.

One shadow. Soft shadows for at most one carried light at a time (the first lit takes them, and gives them up when it goes out): a shadowed point light renders six cube faces of every caster within its range, the cost SiteLightPool already pays for its nearest few indoor lights and no more. Where the quality tier has no additional-light shadows URP draws the light unshadowed and charges nothing for asking.

Presentation only, never simulation: a server never builds one. Attach(Transform, CarriedLightMode) works in edit mode too, so a render probe can hang the same light on its camera and see what a player sees; flicker only runs in play mode.

Remarks

On a camera it is held low and right of the eye; on anything else it is held ahead of the shoulder, measured from the nearest capsule collider at or above the holder (a character's), or from a 1.8 m figure standing at the holder when there is none. Usable outside play mode: the light is lit at once at its mean strength, and the object is never saved with a scene.
