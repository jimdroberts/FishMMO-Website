AI attacking state for caster-focused NPCs (mages, healers, etc.). Keeps maximum distance from the target, prioritizes the longest-range abilities, and retreats aggressively when the target closes in. Stops movement completely while casting for accuracy, and uses wander transitions to reposition when all abilities are on cooldown.

Recommended PreferredDistance of 15-30, MinComfortDistance of 8-12, AttackCooldown of 2-3.
