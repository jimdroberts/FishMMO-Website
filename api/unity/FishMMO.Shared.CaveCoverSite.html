Class CaveCoverSite

Namespace
FishMMO.Shared
Assembly
FishMMO.Shared.dll

What a carved cave's run-time cover is made of (2026-10-20): the cover types it grows (CaveCoverType bits), its density and glow, and the colours its glowing cover takes. Decided at the cut from the cave's biome and form (the editor's CaveCoverSpecies.For) and written on the cave's client-only Visual beside its CaveVisibility, because at run time the client knows neither: the cover renderer (FishMMO.Client CaveCoverSystem) reads it from there.
