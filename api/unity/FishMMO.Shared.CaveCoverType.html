Enum CaveCoverType

Namespace
FishMMO.Shared
Assembly
FishMMO.Shared.dll

The kinds of small, dense cover the run-time cave cover scatters on a carved cave's rock (2026-10-20): the cave's equivalent of the surface's blade grass and detail scatter. Their order is the order of the art's submeshes (SubmeshOf(CaveCoverType, int)) and of the bits of a cave's mask (CaveCoverSite): append only.
