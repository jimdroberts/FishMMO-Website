Remarks

Who tells it what. The character this client owns is told by its own movement (KCCController calls SetGrounded(bool), SetCrouching(bool), SetSwimming(bool, float), SetClimbing(bool, float) each tick) and its own abilities (TriggerAttack(), TriggerCast(), …). Everyone else's movement never runs here: another player is placed by its NetworkTransform (state forwarding is off) and an NPC moves on the server alone. So a character nothing has told anything lately is read from what this client can see (ObservedMotion: the ground under it, the water round it, the climbable it is on), its casts and attacks come from the cast broadcast every observer already receives (AnimateObservedCast(CharacterCastBroadcast, bool)), and its death from the death broadcast (TriggerDeath()). Nothing extra goes on the wire.

The speed is always seen, never told: every body measures how far its model really went each frame, so the feet match the ground under prediction smoothing and interpolation alike.

Client only: a dedicated server compiles none of it (the setters are empty there).

Draws the model half see-through (FishMMO.Shared.CharacterFade) while the character is hidden by /gm invis, or solid again.
