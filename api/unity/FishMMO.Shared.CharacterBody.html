A character model, animated: the clips of its CharacterAnimationSet blended in a PlayableGraph by what the character is doing, at the speed it is really doing it, and the figure set where it belongs (its soles on the ground, its seat on its seat, its head over the waves). The one body behind every character in the game (CharacterAnimationController) and the World Sim walker.

Remarks

A graph, not an AnimatorController. One mixer holds every whole-body state (standing, walking, running, the air, crouching, treading, the two strokes, climbing, sitting, the roll, the death), its weights eased toward the state's and always summing to one. An arms-only layer over it plays the punches, the cast and the guard, so a cast thrown walking keeps the legs walking. There is no controller asset to keep in step with parameters.

Speed-matched. Under the walk clip's own speed idle fades into walk; between walk and run the two are blended with their strides locked to one phase; past the run clip's speed the run plays faster. The strokes play at the swim's pace and the climb is stepped by the height climbed.

In the water as a swimmer is (SwimPose): the movement floats the capsule on still water, so at the surface the figure is set by its head, held just over the drawn sea, riding and tipping with each wave, and it swims head up; only under the surface does it lie flat along its swim.

Client only in use: the server never builds one (the controller's body is compiled out of a server build).

Remarks

On land, placing the model by its bind pose is not enough: a humanoid clip keeps the hip height of the body it was captured on, the capsule hovers its skin width above what it stands on, and on a slope the capsule's lowest point is above the ground under its middle. So the lower of the two soles is found against the ground beneath it and the body eased up or down to meet it, while the figure is on screen. Sat on the ground the soles are on it too, and the same search seats it; on a seat it is raised or lowered by the seat's height against the one the clip was keyed for. In the air it holds where it was, so a landing does not pop.

In the water the capsule floats on the still surface. At the surface the figure is set by its head instead: the head joint held HeadAboveWater over the drawn sea, so the water stands at its chin whether it treads or leans into its stroke, it heaves with every wave, and it tips with the wave's slope. Going under, it eases back onto the capsule, which the controller steers.
