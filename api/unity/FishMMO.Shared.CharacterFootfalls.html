Remarks

From the feet themselves. A body on screen raises a step when one of its foot bones, swinging, comes back down onto the ground under it (FootfallTracker): the sound lands with the foot the player sees land, for any clip and any gait. A body off screen has no bones to watch (its animator is culled), so it raises a step every stride of the distance it goes — still heard behind the camera, a stride apart.

Never from the movement. The owner's controller replays its ticks when it reconciles; a sound raised there would be heard twice. A body is posed once a frame from what it is seen to do, so each footfall is raised once.
