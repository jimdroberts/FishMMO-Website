The climbing movement mode's rules, as pure functions of the climbable, the climber's feet and the tick's controls: the KCC runs them on the server and on the predicting owner alike (and in every replay), so the same inputs give the same climb; the AI walks NPCs along the same geometry; and tests run them with no motor.

Remarks

Stateless but for ClimbState. Where the climber is on the climbable is read from its feet each tick (height up the line, offset across it), never stored, so the reconcile only needs which climbable, the phase and the step's ticks. A rope's side is likewise the side its climber is on.

The feet are the character's pivot (the capsule's bottom, as the KCC places it). A climber hangs hang metres out of the line (the body's radius and ClimbHangClearance), so its capsule never touches the climbable's own line.

Axes(in SceneClimbable, ClimbPhase, Vector3, Vector3, out float, out float)

The climb's axes from the move input in the world: pushing into the climbable climbs it, pulling away goes down, and pushing along it shuffles across. The same push that takes hold (TryAttach(in SceneClimbable, Vector3, Vector3, bool, float, out ClimbPhase, out float)) climbs on, and the one that walks off the top onto a ladder carries on down it, whichever way the camera looks; a climber facing its ladder with the camera behind it climbs on forward and comes down on back, as anyone would expect.
