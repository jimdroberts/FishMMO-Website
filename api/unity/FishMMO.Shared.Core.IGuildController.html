Remarks

The core contract of the Social module's guild (Phase 3, 2026-11-16). Read-only: chat, the map, nameplates, housing access, the harm rule and observer streaming ask "which guild?" and "same guild?", and none of them change it. Joining, ranks, the roster window and the guild system are the module's (IGuildMembership). Without the module no character has this, and every one of those reads finds no guild.
