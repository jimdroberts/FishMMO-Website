The standing mark a plate carries in its icon slot when it has no icon of its own: what the viewer is to this character, at a glance and without relying on colour alone.

public enum NameplateMark : byte

Fields

Friendly = 3

A shield: party, guild, or an arena teammate.

Hostile = 1

Crossed swords: may be fought (an enemy, or a player with aggression on, the viewer included).

Neutral = 2

A lozenge: neither friend nor foe.

None = 0

No mark.

Remarks

The art is the client's (UINameplates.uss, one class per mark); the plate only says which.
