Remarks

The level is quantised to sixteen bits rather than sent as a float. Two reasons, and the second is the important one. It is smaller on the wire; and because Quantise(float) is applied on BOTH sides — the server quantises what it sends, the owner quantises what it restores — the two hold bit-identical levels immediately after every reconcile, instead of differing by the rounding and re-diverging from there. A state at 1/65535 resolution is far finer than any buff threshold cares about.

Producers MUST emit entries sorted by TemplateID ascending, like every other array in CharacterReconcileData: the index-delta serializer compares position by position, so an unstable order would send every entry every tick.
