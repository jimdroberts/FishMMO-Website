StepMetres(float, float)

The length of one step (half a stride), metres, at a ground speed, for a body 1.8 m tall: about 0.75 m at a walk (1.4 m/s, 1.9 steps a second), 1.4 m at a run (4 m/s), 1.9 m at a sprint (6.5 m/s). A taller body steps further in proportion.
