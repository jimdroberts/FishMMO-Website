Why the group finder refused, or ended, a character's request.

public enum GroupFinderRefusalReason : byte

Fields

EnteredInstance = 11

Removed from the queue because the character entered an instance by other means.

GroupLeftWithoutYou = 12

A group formed, but the character stayed untransferable — in combat, dead — for longer than the server allows, and the group went on without them.

HoldsInstance = 6

The character, or a party they were in, still holds an open instance.

InInstance = 4

The character is already inside instanced content.

InParty = 5

The character is in a party with other people. Matching joins a party the finder builds, so the player must leave theirs first — or have its leader open the dungeon to others, which is how a partial group fills its empty slots.

JoinedParty = 10

Removed from the queue because the character joined a party by other means.

Left = 9

The player left the queue.

LeftEntrance = 14

Removed from the queue because the character walked away from the entrance. Waiting is done standing at the dungeon, so that being moved into it is never a surprise.

NoEntrance = 1

The entrance no longer exists, or the player has walked away from it.

None = 0

Nothing to explain.

NotAvailable = 3

Find Group is not offered at this difficulty, or the dungeon cannot seat a group.

NotPartyLeader = 15

Only the party leader may queue the party for an arena.

OnCooldown = 7

Asked again too soon.

PartyMemberBusy = 18

A party member is inside an instance, holds one, or is seated in a live arena match.

PartyMustFillTeam = 20

Ranked arenas: a pre-made party must fill a whole team of the format.

PartyNotPresent = 17

A party member is not at the board, or not on this scene server.

PartyTooLarge = 16

The party has more members than a team of the chosen format seats.

QueueLocked = 19

Locked out of the arena queue for deserting a match or declining a ready check.

Removed = 13

The queue entry disappeared server-side: a server restart, or a stale-row sweep.

ServerError = 8

The server could not complete the request; try again.

UnknownDifficulty = 2

The dungeon does not offer the difficulty that was asked for.
