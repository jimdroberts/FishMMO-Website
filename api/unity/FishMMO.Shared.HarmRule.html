Who may harm whom: the one rule every hostile effect answers to, so an ability needs no authored condition to leave friends alone. Asked by damage (Damage(ICharacter, int, DamageAttributeTemplate, bool, bool)) and, through CharacterEvade, by debuffs, knockback, interrupts, taunts, buff-stripping dispels and resource drains.

Remarks

The rule itself is Core; what decides it is supplied by features as IHarmRelations, asked in order until one decides. Shipped: the arena's match sides (only an opponent, whatever anyone's toggle says), wildlife (outside every other relation), companions (party, guild, one's own pet: never), and faction standing with the aggression toggles. With no relation deciding, or none installed, a hit is allowed: combat works without any of them.

No attacker (the world: a fall, lava), or the character harming itself, is never restricted. A pet answers as its owner on either side (Principal(ICharacter)).
