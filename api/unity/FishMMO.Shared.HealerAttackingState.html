AI attacking state for healer-focused NPCs. Prioritises healing injured allies before dealing damage. Scans for nearby allies each update, selects the most injured one whose health is below HealThreshold, and uses a heal ability on them. Falls back to normal attack behaviour when no ally needs healing.

Abilities are considered "heal abilities" when their template ID appears in HealAbilityTemplateIDs. All other abilities are treated as damage.

Recommended PreferredDistance of 15-25, MinComfortDistance of 6-10.
