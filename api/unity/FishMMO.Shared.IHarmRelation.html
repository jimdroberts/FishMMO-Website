One feature's say in who may harm whom: the arena's teams, wildlife, companions (party, guild, one's own pet), faction standing. HarmRule asks the relations in Order; the first that decides wins.

public interface IHarmRelation

Remarks

A feature declares its relation with [assembly: HarmRelation(typeof(MyRelation))], so removing the feature (a module's folder) removes its say and nothing else: with no relation deciding, a hit is allowed. Relations are asked with each side's principal (Principal(ICharacter): a pet answers as its owner).

Properties

True when this relation's refusal is lifted by the attacking side turning its aggression toggle on (the factions' "not an enemy" refusal). False for refusals no toggle changes: an arena's teams, a pack, companions.
