Where the player's camera sits relative to their character.

public enum KCCCameraStyle : byte

Fields

FirstPerson = 2

At the character's eyes.

OverShoulder = 1

Close behind and to the right of the character, looking past their shoulder.

ThirdPerson = 0

Centred behind the character, orbiting and zooming freely. The original camera.

Remarks

Stored as a player setting (ClientCameraSettings), so the values are ours and never reordered: a saved file keeps its meaning across releases. The order the Options dropdown shows them in is separate.

The character faces the camera's heading in every style (OrientationMethod.TowardsCamera); the styles differ only in where the view is taken from. Where the shots go is the same rule in all three: from the character's eye to the point under the crosshair (AimConvergence), which is what lets the view sit off the character's line at all.
