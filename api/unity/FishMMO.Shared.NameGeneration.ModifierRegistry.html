Runtime index of every loaded NameModifierTemplate, keyed by its normalised modifier key. A modifier is an orthogonal flavour applied on top of any race and culture; with 30 races, 3 cultures each and 10 modifiers that is 900 name profiles from roughly the authoring cost of 30.
