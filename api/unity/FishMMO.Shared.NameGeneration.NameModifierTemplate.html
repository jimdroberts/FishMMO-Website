FishMMO.Shared.NameGeneration

A cross-race flavour — ashen, bloodborn, tidebound — layered on top of any race's phonology. Its syllables are merged into the base phonology so names lean toward the theme without losing the race's sound. Registers itself with ModifierRegistry when loaded.
