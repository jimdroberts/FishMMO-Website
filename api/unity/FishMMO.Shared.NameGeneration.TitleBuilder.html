Template-driven title builder.

A title is a TitleTemplate from the grammar asset with its slots filled from the race's tables (honorifics, epithets, ranks, legends, occupations, places) and the grammar's universal vocabulary (deeds, eras, ordinals…). Templates whose slots cannot be filled for a race are skipped; honorifics only take a place or an ordinal when the grammar says they may; honorifics follow the character's gender; and a length budget decides whether a second clause is appended at all.

Examples: Sir · Lady of Ashford · King, Third of his Name · the Ironwilled · Master Coinkeeper of Kingshold · Who Sealed the Dark Gate at the Battle of Hollow Hill.
