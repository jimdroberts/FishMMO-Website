Remarks

Two limits apply. A character has the pet slots its Pet Slots character attribute gives it (PetSlots: one unless an archetype, an item or a buff raises it), and each summon spell carries its own maximum (MaxPets): a spell that may raise three wolves still raises one for a character with one slot.

A summon past either limit replaces the oldest pet in its way rather than failing: past the spell's limit, the oldest pet of that spell; past the slots, the oldest pet of any kind. That is what a summon did when a character could only have one pet.

A pet keeps the slot it was given for as long as it is out, and the database stores each pet under its slot. A slot is not a list position: when the first of two pets dies the second stays in slot 1.
