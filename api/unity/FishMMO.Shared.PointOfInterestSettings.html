Remarks

Saved beside the scene's atlas entry, not in its terrain folder, so it exists before the first cut and a re-cut (which clears the terrain folder) keeps it. The atlas entry points at it (WorldAtlasScene.PointsOfInterest), and the scene's ScenePointOfInterestSettings shows it in the inspector.
