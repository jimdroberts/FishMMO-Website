Remarks

One flat pad on a hillside cut its uphill half into the hill and stood its downhill half on fill, both as deep as the hill fell across it, and banked out a hundred metres to meet the slope. Terraced, each level is cut and filled only by the fall across its own band, and the town climbs the hill as old hill towns do.

The levels are bands across TerraceAxis (up the hill), split at TerraceSteps (metres along it from the pad's centre). Each step's bank rises at BankGrade (42°, just under what is walked), except across RampHalfWidth either side of its ramp (TerraceRamps, metres across the axis), where it is graded at RampGrade for a cart, its sides cut at the bank's grade.
