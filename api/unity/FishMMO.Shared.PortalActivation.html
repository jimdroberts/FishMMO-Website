Remarks

The rules (2026-10-10). A portal is inactive until activated. A player activates it by using it while meeting ANY of its Conditions (an ability known, an item held, the placeholder HasProfessionCondition) or holding its Key; the key is designer-assigned and generated portals leave it empty. Who it then opens for is Scope, chosen per portal: the activating character only, everyone for DurationSeconds, or everyone for good. The rules are PortalActivationRules; the use is PortalGate, called by TeleportAction and SceneTeleporter before they move anyone.

Identity is (scene, PortalIndex). The index is the bit a character's activation record stores and the key of the world row, so it must be unique in its scene and never change once the scene ships — the waypoint contract, in its own index space (a waypoint and a portal may both be 0).

Look. On a client, ActiveVisual / InactiveVisual are switched from what the server reported (PortalClientStates). Both optional.
