Remarks

Called by TeleportAction (a Teleporter interaction) and SceneTeleporter (walking into a scene portal) just before they move the player. An object without a PortalActivation is not gated at all, so every teleporter that existed before portals keeps working unchanged.

Activation IS the use: there is no separate "activate" interaction. A player who meets a condition walks straight through, and the portal stays open for whoever its scope says. Every refusal answers the owner with the general ToastBroadcast, the same path every other teleport refusal takes.
