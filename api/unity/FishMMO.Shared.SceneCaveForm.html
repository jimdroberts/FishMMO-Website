The kind of cave a spot is in, which says what can live there (the cut's cave forms, by the same ordinals).

public enum SceneCaveForm : byte

Fields

Cave = 0

A natural cave in the hill's rock: the richest life.

Grotto = 1

A short cave opening onto water, its front flooded.

IceCave = 3

A cave in ice: nearly lifeless.

LavaTube = 4

A drained lava conduit: dry bare basalt, a few hardy crawlers.

Mine = 5

A worked mine: its drifts and stopes.

SeaCave = 2

A cave the sea runs into.

SeaHall = 6

A sea hall under an ice shell (2026-10-26, I2n-b): its life is its sea's, swimming in its water.

Remarks

Append only; the ordinal is stored in scene assets.
