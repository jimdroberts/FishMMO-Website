What a cave-life spot is: the kind of place in a cave an animal lives on, in or over.

public enum SceneCaveLifeKind : byte

Fields

Brine = 9

A hot brine pool on a sea hall's floor (2026-10-26, I2n-b): Position its mirror's middle (the brine's surface under the sea water), Extent x its radius, y how far its rim stands over the mirror. Drawn by the client as a shimmering mirror; nothing lives in it.

Flight = 3

A box of open air found clear of rock all through (Extent its half-extents along Yaw, up and across): bats and moths fly loops in it.

Floor = 0

A patch of floor flat enough to walk on (Extent.x across): rats, crickets, salamanders.

Glow = 6

Still damp air under a high roof, where glow-worms hang their threads and fungus gnats dance.

Hall = 8

A sea hall's dome (2026-10-26, I2n-b): Position its vault's centre (an ellipsoid on a flat floor), Extent its half-extents along Yaw, up and across: the roof the client's brinicles hang from and its smoke ceiling spreads under. Its water is the hall's Pool spot.

Mouth = 5

A way out to the sky: Position in the mouth's bore, Normal out of it, and a straight run back in clear of rock (Extent.z): what bats stream out along at dusk and back in along before dawn.

Pool = 4

The still water standing in a cave (a grotto's pool, a sea cave's tide), at its surface: olms swim under it.

Roost = 2

A patch of roof high over the floor, its normal pointing down: where a bat colony hangs.

Smoker = 7

A sea hall's smoker (2026-10-26, phase I2n-b, decision 83; its kind in SceneCaveLifeFlags): Position its orifice, Extent x the orifice's radius, y the chimney's height under it, z the clear water and air over it to the roof; Yaw its own turn (where its flanges face). What the client's plumes rise from (SceneSeaHallVents); nothing lives on it.

Wall = 1

A patch of wall, its normal level or nearly: geckos, spiders and crickets cling to it.
