One place in a carved cave its animals live at, baked by the cut from the cave's own field (the cave dressing): a flat patch of floor, wall or roof, a box of clear air, a pool, a mouth. The ambient life places its cave creatures on these and keeps them to them, so it needs no ray against the cave's rock, and the cave's floors under and over each other are no puzzle to it (the terrain's one height a place is useless underground).

Remarks

A patch is flat. A floor, wall or roof patch was measured round its centre and kept only as wide as the rock lies within a few centimetres of its plane, so whatever walks or hangs on it, anywhere within Extent.x of Position on the plane through it square to Normal, stands on the rock.

Plain data: read in builds, by the client only (nothing here is gameplay).
