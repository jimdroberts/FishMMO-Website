What lives on and in a cave's still water as every client draws it (2026-10-26, phase I2n-c; decision 86 (i) and its leeches, decision 97): marsh-gas BEADS rising in trails from the drowned muck and ringing out on the surface, and LEECHES swimming in it, toward whoever wades in. Pure; FishCaveWaterLife.shader mirrors it number for number (CaveWaterLife draws it).

Remarks

The beads are the shared clock's. A pool's vents are hashed from its surface's position (to the centimetre: every client loads the same cave-life spot), each releasing a burst of beads every Periods seconds (each period a whole number of cycles in the motion clock's Wrap, so a trail never jumps when it wraps), a burst kept or skipped by its own hash: every player sees the same bead break the surface at the same moment.

The leeches are each viewer's. At rest a leech's slow circuit of its pool is the clock's as the beads are; how far it has turned toward a wader is a presentation each client eases on its own (Attract(float, bool, float)): nothing on the wire, no damage, nothing anyone else sees moves (decision 97: viewer-reactive effects only as per-viewer presentation).
