One ladder, rope ladder or rope a character can climb, baked by the cut (a cave's shafts and ledges, a mine's levels): the climbing movement mode, the server's NavMesh links and the NPCs' traversal of them all read these, nothing else. The pieces drawn for it are baked props, data like this, so nothing here refers to them.

Remarks

The climb is the segment Bottom to Top, its centre line. A climber's feet hang Out of it by the body's radius and a little more, and are kept on that line's height: it may lean (a ladder against a ledge, up to about 15 degrees) but never far from upright.

Identified at run time by its index in Climbables within the character's own scene: the server and every client load the same asset, so the index is the same on both and is what the reconcile carries.
