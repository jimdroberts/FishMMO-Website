A scene cut on a gas giant (Celestial.CloudWorld): islands of rock floating over a cloud deck with nothing under it. Written by the cut, kept by the server and every client, and found by scene (TryGet(Scene, out SceneCloudWorld)) by what has to know: the sky (the deck it draws below the islands), the server (who has fallen into it), the airships (the docks they sail between).

Remarks

Heights are y, as everywhere else: scene metres above the body's datum. The deck's top is at DeckTop, the islands stand between IslandFloor and IslandCeiling, and a character that sinks past DeathAltitude is lost in the cloud: it dies, and respawns as from any death.

Keyed by scene, as SceneClimbables is: a scene server runs several world scenes at once, each in its own physics scene, and a character asks with its own.
