Water dripping from a cave's roof (2026-10-20, caves livened; Drip): how often a drip falls, how its drops fall, when each lands, and how its seed carries the puddle under it. One definition read by the client's drops and puddle rings (CaveDrips and its shader, which repeats FallenMetres(float), JitterOf(uint, long) and the ring constants), its sound (CaveDripPresenter) and the cave dressing that bakes the drips.

Remarks

Shared by every player. Drop k of a drip lands at (k + jitter(k)) · interval + phase seconds of the shared motion clock (WorldMotion.Seconds), the jitter a hash of the drip and k within ±Jitter of the interval: irregular, as a real drip is, yet the same moment on every screen and in every ear. Nothing is integrated frame to frame; ClockAt(double, uint, out long, out float) reads the clock in double precision, so the drops never drift however long the world has run.

The fall. A drop of 4–5 mm falls with the air's drag on it: v(t) = vt tanh(g t / vt), so y(t) = (vt² / g) ln cosh(g t / vt), close to free fall over the first metre or two and at its terminal TerminalSpeed down a high vault's thirty. The cave dressing keeps every drip's interval at least FallSeconds(float) / InFlight, so no more than three of its drops are ever in the air at once.
