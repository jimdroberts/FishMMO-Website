What a site's piece gives off where it stands (StructurePieces.EmittersOf).

public enum SceneEmitterKind : byte

Fields

Beacon = 9

A lighthouse's lantern (2026-10-20): a great fire behind polished bronze mirrors that a keeper's clockwork turns, lit from dusk. Its warm light fills the lantern and gallery (SiteLightPool); its beams sweep the sea and the land round it, seen for kilometres, and flash when one passes over the eye (LighthouseBeams). How fast it turns and how many beams it throws come from the seed, so each lighthouse is known by its own light.

Crystal = 6

A cluster of glowing crystals deep in a cave: a cold, steady light, day and night alike (no sky reaches it), its hue from bits 8–9 of the seed (violet, blue, green, blue), reaching Reach.

Drip = 12

Water dripping from a cave's roof (2026-10-20, caves livened): its place is where the drops land (a puddle's surface, the floor or a stalagmite's tip), Reach is how far they fall, and its seed how often they fall and the puddle round it (SceneDrip). No light of its own: the drops, the rings in the puddle and the sound are on the shared clock (CaveDripField, CaveDripPresenter).

Fire = 1

An open fire's bed: firelight, flicker and smoke.

Forge = 2

A forge's hearth: a hot, low glow.

Fumes = 11

Thin sulphur fumes hanging under a lava tube's roof over its lava (2026-10-20): a slow drift of haze lit orange from below, no light of its own, spread over Reach metres round its place (SiteEmbers).

Glow = 8

A cold glow of faerie, rune or crystal light (2026-10-18, I6g: a glowjar, a rune stone, a crystal post): a cool tint from bits 8–10 of the seed (SceneGlow: a crystal's hue, rune-light or faerie-light), a soft slow pulse on the shared world clock, faintly showing from dusk and full at night (_FishGlowLights), day and night alike underground. Its piece's glowing parts pulse with it (FishStructureWeathering.hlsl, a material marked _FishStructure = 3).

Hearth = 4

A fire indoors (a hearth in a room): flicker and sparks, and no smoke — its smoke goes up the chimney. Its light is the room's (Indoor).

Indoor = 5

A lived-in room's light (its hearth and candles), at the room's middle and reaching only its walls (Reach): the outside of the walls faces away from it, so a house is not lit through them.

Lamp = 3

A lantern's pane: a steady warm light at night.

Magma = 10

Molten lava in a cave (2026-10-20, a lava tube that still runs: its river, lake or fall), just over its surface: a deep orange light, lit day and night, churning slowly on the shared clock (SceneMagma), reaching Reach; the embers that rise off it and the heat haze over it (SiteEmbers, the heat shimmer). Its seed carries the body's half-width and kind (Seed(uint, float, SceneMagmaBody)).

Pyre = 14

A way light's own open fire whose look its seed carries (ScenePyre, 2026-10-26): a beacon's pyre on a tower top or a hill, dark by day and kindled at dusk, each at its own moment of the dusk on the shared clock, burning all night; or a brimstone lamp's low blue sulphur flame. Lit by the site lights (SiteLightPool), its flames and sparks by SiteEmbers; no mirrors, so no beams (a lighthouse's lantern is Beacon).

Skylight = 13

An opening in a cave's roof up to the sky (2026-10-20, caves livened): its place is the lit floor under the opening, Reach the opening's radius, and its seed how far up the opening is (SceneSkylight). The daylight's shaft and the motes in it, the sky let in round it (CaveSkylights, FishSkylights.hlsl) and a soft light off the sunlit floor by day (SiteLightPool); nothing at night but the moon's.

Smoke = 0

A chimney's top: hearth smoke.

Torch = 7

A lantern or torch underground (a mine's lamps, a cave camp's): a warm flicker, lit day and night, reaching Reach.
