The cold glow of faerie, rune and crystal light (2026-10-18, I6g; Glow): its tint, its soft slow pulse and when it shows. One definition read by the client's site lights (SiteLightPool) and, constant for constant, by the glowing parts of the pieces that give it off (FishStructureWeathering.hlsl FishStructureGlowLight), so a rune stone's runes brighten and dim with the light they cast.

Remarks

Shared by every player. The pulse is a pure function of the shared motion clock (WorldMotion, wrapped at WrapSeconds as the shaders' _FishWeatherMisc.w is) and the glow's place: no per-client state, so two players side by side see the same rune brighten at the same instant. Each beat's rate is a whole number of cycles in the wrap, so the pulse meets itself across it instead of jumping. Its phase is a smooth field over the ground (two slow waves), not a hash, so a light and the stone it stands in front of — half a metre apart — pulse as one, while glows down a street breathe one after another.

When. Lamps light at dusk (SkySystem's _FishNightLights, as the sun goes below 7°); a glow already shows, faintly, from the sun at 19° (Visibility(float), published as _FishGlowLights), and is full in the night. Underground it is full day and night, as a buried lamp is.

Flicker (2026-10-18, decision 33: stormglass, a crystal full of cloud with threads of lightning in it). A glow whose seed carries FlickerBit crackles instead of breathing (Flicker(float, float, float)): a low steady glow with bright, uneven flashes, the same pure function of the shared clock and the place, so the crystal on a post and the light it casts flash together while the next post's flash at their own moments. Its parts' material flickers by the same constants (FishStructureWeathering.hlsl FishGlowFlicker).
