The molten lava of a lava tube that still runs (2026-10-20; Magma and Fumes): its light's colour and slow churn, how its seed carries the body under it, how much it sparks, the hot air over it and the haze under the roof. One definition read by the client's site lights (SiteLightPool), its embers and fumes (SiteEmbers), its heat haze (the heat shimmer) and the cave walk render.

Remarks

Shared by every player. The churn is a pure function of the shared motion clock (WorldMotion, wrapped at WrapSeconds as the shaders' _FishWeatherMisc.w is) and the light's place, each beat a whole number of cycles in the wrap, as SceneGlow's pulse is: two players beside one river see it surge together.

The colour is the light of lava's own glow: a crust's cracks at 1000–1150 °C shine a deep orange (blackbody ~1300 K, little blue), a fall's torn, fresher surface a little yellower. Underground no sky outshines it, so it burns the same by day as by night.
