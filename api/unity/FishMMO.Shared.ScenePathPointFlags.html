Per-point facts about a path (Flags).

[Flags]
public enum ScenePathPointFlags : byte

Fields

Boardwalk = 32

On a boardwalk (2026-10-18): a segment between two such points is walked on a plank deck on piles laid along it, so, as under a bridge's deck, nothing is drawn or carved on the ground beneath (Biomes.BiomeBoardwalks).

Bridge = 1

On a bridge's deck: nothing is drawn or carved on the ground under it.

Ferry = 64

On a rope ferry's crossing (decision 35 (i), 2026-10-18): a segment between two such points is travelled on the ferry flat, so, as under a bridge's deck, nothing is drawn or carved on the river's bed beneath.

Floor = 16

A segment between two such points is a building's floor: within it nothing drawn over it wears the ground less (ScenePathField), so no grass or detail comes up indoors whatever path or apron crosses it.

Ford = 2

Wading a ford: the bed is left as the river made it, the banks ramped down to it.

InSite = 4

Inside a site's footprint (a settlement's gate, a ruin's court): the pad already shaped the ground.

None = 0
Squared = 8

A segment between two such points is drawn as a rectangle from point to point, its half width either side: a building's plan or a ploughed plot, whose corners a round-ended strip left as grass. The points are the rectangle's end midpoints, so never one point, which would give it no direction.

Stairs = 128

On a straight road's stair (2026-10-26, phase I2l-b): a segment between two such points climbs a cliff the road keeps its line over, cut as one even flight from the foot to the top (PathCarver) rather than evened to the road's grade.
