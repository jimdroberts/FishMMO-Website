For(ScenePathClass)

The numbers for each class. Grades after real practice: a paved highway keeps under about 8 %, a carriage road 11 %, a cart 15 %; a footpath takes a quarter and a hill trail the steepest a walker climbs without hands. Widths: a single file trail 0.8 m, a footpath 1.3 m, a cart's gauge with its verges 3 m, a road two carts.

For(ScenePathClass, ScenePathForm)

A way's numbers by its class and form (Form, 2026-10-26, phase I2l-b): a routed way's are its class's exactly (For(ScenePathClass)). A straight road evens its line over twice its class's length and cuts and fills up to 4 m to hold its grade; a raised one stands RaisedMetres over the ground between short steep sides; a cut one lies CutMetres down between near-sheer walls. The trodden width, surface and grade are the class's.

SurfaceCode(ScenePathSurface)

The surface as the shader's code: track 0, earth ⅓, gravel ⅔, stone 1. Where two ways meet the field blends their codes, so the order is the order a blend passes through: the track (earth with ruts and a grass median) sits at the end, where the bake's first order (earth 0, track ⅓) put it between earth and gravel — a footpath running into a road grew a stretch of ruts and median grass at the seam (field encoding 2, Encoding).
