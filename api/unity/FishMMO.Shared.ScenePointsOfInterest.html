Remarks

The scene's ScenePointOfInterest objects, the "POI" prop set, the spawners, the world map's entries and the atlas's view are all built from this. The PLAN (pads, keep-outs, cave descriptors) is kept too, because the plan changes the ground: a repaint reads it back instead of planning again on ground that the first plan already flattened.

Fields

CaveLife

Where the caves' animals live (SceneCaveLifeSpot): flat patches of their floors, walls and roofs, boxes of clear air, pools, mouths, baked by the cave dressing from each system's field. Read by the client's ambient life only (SceneCaveLife); a scene cut before them has none, and its caves are empty until it is cut again.

public List<SceneCaveLifeSpot> CaveLife

Climbables

The ladders, rope ladders and ropes the sites' caves and mines were given, as laid (SceneClimbable). Read on the server as well as the client: climbing is predicted movement the server is the authority for, and the server's NavMesh links its NPCs climb come from these (SceneClimbables).

PathEarthMap

Per terrain layer (array slice), the slice of its biome's own path ground (footpaths, trails, tracks), road ground and cobbles, −1 for none: the overlay wears a way into the ground of the biome under each pixel, and paves its cobbled stretches with that biome's stone. 32 entries each. A scene cut before the cobbles had a map (2026-10-15) has none, and paves with PathLayers.z everywhere until it is repainted.
