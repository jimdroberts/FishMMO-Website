A way light's own open fire (Pyre, 2026-10-26, the biome suite's way lights): what its seed says of it and how bright it burns. One definition read by the client's site lights (SiteLightPool) and its flames and sparks (SiteEmbers), so the light a beacon throws and the flames over it are kindled at the same instant.

Remarks

Its seed. The low 16 bits are the flicker's phase, as every fire's (a hash of where it stands); bit 16 marks a beacon (DuskBit: kindled at dusk), bit 17 a brimstone flame (BlueBit: burning blue). The site writes both bits from its piece (StructurePieces.PyreLookOf), never the hash's.

Kindled at dusk, on the shared clock. A beacon's fire is dark by day: it is lit as the lamps are, once the sun is low enough (SkySystem's _FishNightLights, a pure function of the sun's height and so of the shared world clock), each beacon at its own moment of the dusk (KindleAt(uint), from its seed), so a line of beacons along a border is seen being lit one after another as the dusk deepens, the same for every player. It burns all night and goes out as the dawn comes back up through the same moment.
