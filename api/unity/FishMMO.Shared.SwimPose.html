How a swimmer is drawn in the water: how far it leans, how near the surface it counts as, where its body turns, and how it tips on a wave. The arithmetic behind CharacterAnimationController's procedural swim pose and the World Sim walker's figure, kept apart so both pose a swimmer the same way and the rules can be tested.

Remarks

Presentation only. The movement (KCCController) floats the capsule on the still water, a fixed depth under it, and never sees a wave. Nothing here feeds back into it.

Leans are degrees forward of upright, as a model's local pitch: 0 stands, 90 lies flat along the swim, past 90 dives. Unity turns +X pitch nose down (forward (0, 0, 1) goes to (0, −sin θ, cos θ)), so a nose-up tilt is negative.

The capsule floats with its feet SwimFloatSubmersion of its 1.6 m height (1.2 m) under the still water, so a body turned about this point lies just under the surface (0.1 m). Turned about its feet, a level swimmer lay the whole 1.2 m down.
