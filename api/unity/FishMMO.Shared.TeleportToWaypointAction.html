Remarks

Goes through TryTravel(IPlayerCharacter, IWaypoint, in WaypointTravelOptions, out WaypointTravelRefusalReason), the same routine the map's fast-travel request uses, so the rules are the same; the options below let a designer relax the two that are policy rather than physics (discovery, combat). The waypoint's own travel conditions are a third: an item that bypasses a level gate is a legitimate thing to author.

Same-scene only for now. The waypoint is resolved in the character's own scene instance; a SceneName naming another scene is refused with a warning until the world-map transfer lands.
