The ground colour map (GroundColourMap) over all of the scene's terrains: per texel the splat-weighted mean colour of the layers as the terrain draws them, and the surface normal; with what it was made from, so the client uses it only when the same terrains stand in the same places drawn with the same texture sets.

Shares

RGBA8 texels: what the ground there is made of for the planet's looks (r living, g lichen, b mineral, by the splat weights; a coverage). The colour above is the terrain's own; the client wears the world's looks over it by these at load (GroundColourMap.WearLooks), so the bake depends on no world and holds for the looks on or off. Empty in a bake made before it: the client then works the map out itself.
