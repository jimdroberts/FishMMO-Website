Which biome's spawn rule made each of a terrain's detail prototypes, beside the terrain (written by the scene cut's scatter, TerrainScatter). The scatter makes one prototype per rule and prefab, so the grass on a terrain is several prototypes of one prefab, each painted where its own biome grows it; with this the client knows which biome's look a prototype's blades take (Biomes.BiomeTemplate.GrassTypes), and that its healthy and dry tints are its rule's.

Remarks

Keyed by the prototype's prefab name and noise seed, not its index: the scatter seeds every prototype from its rule (a distinct seed per rule and prefab), both survive any copy of the prototype, and a prototype added afterwards (a point of interest's wheat, a hand-painted one) simply has no entry. By name rather than by reference so the record pulls no prefab into a build the terrain's own prototypes do not (the server strips those).

A biome is its cached-object id (Biomes.BiomeRegistry.IDOf), as the scene's biome map stores it, never a reference: a scene reference to a template would pull its art into the scene's bundle.

A point of interest's prototypes (a herb grove's herbs and flowers, a cave mouth's growth, a field's wheat: prototypes a site adds to a tile after the scatter) are recorded too since 2026-10-16, for the biome the site stands in, marked Site: they take that biome's look of the prefab (or the old look where it has none), and keep their material's tints, as they had before, since no rule of the biome chose them.

A site paints its own biome's prototype (2026-10-16, I4b): it reuses a tile's prototype of the plant only when that prototype is its own biome's (a rule's or an earlier site's, SiteLayer(string, int, IReadOnlyList<string>, IReadOnlyList<int>, IReadOnlyList<Entry>, out int)); where the tile's prototype of the plant is another biome's, the site adds one of its own (a copy of that one, with a noise seed of its own, SiteSeed(int, int, string, IReadOnlyList<string>, IReadOnlyList<int>, IReadOnlyList<Entry>)), so the plant takes the look of the biome the site stands in rather than whichever biome's prototype came first. Its cover goes on as before, measured against the plant's prototype it used to paint into (SiteCover(int, int, bool, int)), so a plant is no thicker for being split between two prototypes.

The cover a site leaves in its own prototype of a plant at one detail cell, where it wants want and grows only ever more: measured, as it always was, against the plant's prototype it used to paint into (the tile's first of the plant, baseCover) together with its own (ownCover), and only the difference added to its own. sameLayer: its own is that first one (the tile had no other biome's prototype of the plant first), so the cell is simply raised to want.
