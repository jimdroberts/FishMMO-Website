The water of a scene, for the simulation: where a character swims, how deep an agent would have to go, what drowns or burns. Keyed by scene, as WeatherQuery is, and answered at a synced server tick, so the server and a predicting client get the identical answer.

Remarks

Not SurfaceWater. That is the weather's view of the scene being drawn: global, with no scene key (a scene server runs many scenes at once), and its sea moves with the frame's clock and its waves. This answers from what both peers hold alike: the sea's SeaTide at a tick, and the scene's lakes and rivers (SceneWaterBodies), which never move.

The sea is everywhere. A sea's level is returned at any place in its scene: whether the ground there stands above it is the caller's to compare, since a character knows its ground and this does not. A lake or a river stands only where it is, and the highest water over a place answers. An inland lake or river of lava (SurfaceWater.ILavaAreaSource) answers as Lava, as a lava sea does.

Positive infinity for y is the highest over the place.
