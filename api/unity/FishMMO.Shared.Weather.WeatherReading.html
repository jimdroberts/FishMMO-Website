Remarks

Predicted exposure, buff volumes, AI shelter and spawn conditions read this through WeatherQuery; the Climate and Weather module produces it (its full WeatherSample carries the simulation's own types too). With no weather installed every reading is Calm: nothing falls, no wind, a mild temperature, open air.
