Namespace FishMMO.Shared.Weather

Classes

ShelterQuery

Where an NPC can get out of the weather (Phase 4 of the core and modules plan, 2026-11-18): the nearest shelter volume's bounds. The World Simulation module installs Source from its weather volumes; with none installed there is no shelter anywhere, and an NPC that would seek one stays where it is.

SurfaceWater

The open water in the loaded scene — a sea, its lakes and rivers: where anything falling out of the sky stops, whether a point is under a surface, and how high the water stands at a place.

WaterQuery

The water of a scene, for the simulation: where a character swims, how deep an agent would have to go, what drowns or burns. Keyed by scene, as WeatherQuery is, and answered at a synced server tick, so the server and a predicting client get the identical answer.

WeatherChannels

The channel table: counts, blend rules and helpers.

WeatherExposureIngredient

One exposure state a recipe needs, and how far into it the character has to be.

WeatherExposureRecipe

A state that exists only where two or more others do at once: soaked AND chilled is frozen, which is not what either one alone means.

WeatherExposureTemplate

A state the weather can put a character into — wet, chilled, wind-burnt — and the buff that goes with it. Static content: what the weather has to be doing, how long it takes to build and to wear off, and what it applies. Never any runtime state; the levels live on WeatherExposureController, one set per character.

WeatherExposureTerm

One input's contribution to an exposure state.

WeatherExposureTick

Converts a prediction replicate's tick into the tick the weather timeline is evaluated at.

WeatherQuery

The weather at a place and tick, for gameplay: the core's question, answered by whichever weather is installed.

Structs

WaterSample

The water over a place at a tick.

WeatherAir

What the air is doing at a place and a moment: the synoptic state the weather driver computes.

WeatherFrame

One value per WeatherChannel. A value type with named fields so sampling weather for every player every second allocates nothing.

WeatherReading

The weather at a place and tick, as gameplay reads it: the frame of channels, the air, the temperature and the shelter. Core vocabulary (Phase 4 of the core and modules plan, 2026-11-17).

Interfaces

ISeaLevel

A scene's sea as the water contract needs it: its level at a tick. SeaTide (the tide driven by the world's moons) implements it; a game without that module can register a flat sea.

SurfaceWater.IAreaSource

Water that stands only in some places: lakes and rivers.

SurfaceWater.IDaylightSource

A sea that dims the daylight on its way down to its floor (2026-10-25, phase I2m, decision 36 (c)): how much per metre, the scene cut's (BiomeSeaFloors.DaylightExtinction); 0 for none.

SurfaceWater.IFlowingAreaSource

Area water some of which may be molten lava (a lava tube's river, a pit in its chamber): which the highest surface over a place is, so what lives, wades, splashes or floats in water can leave lava alone.

SurfaceWater.ILavaAreaSource
SurfaceWater.ISource

What a body of open water tells the weather about itself.

SurfaceWater.IStackedAreaSource

Area water that stands at more than one level over a place (2026-10-21: a cave's stream under a lava tube's river on the level over it, a lake on the hill over both): which surface a height is in or over, not only the highest.

SurfaceWater.IWaveSource

Water whose drawn surface moves (the sea's waves): what a floating body is set on to ride them.
