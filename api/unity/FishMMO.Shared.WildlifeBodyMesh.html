Remarks

The animal faces +Z, stands on y = 0 and is centred on x = 0.

The vertex contract: colour rgb is the albedo (back, belly, accent, horn); UV1 xyz is the pivot of the vertex's part in mesh space and w its part (WildlifeBodyMesh.Part). The shader swings each leg about its hip, the head group about the neck base and the tail about its root.
