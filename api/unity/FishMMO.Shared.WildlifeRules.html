FleeHeading(Vector3, IReadOnlyList<Vector3>, Vector3)

The flat direction prey should run: away from every threat at once, each weighted by how close it is (1/d), so a deer between two wolves runs out sideways rather than from one into the other. Pure, so it is tested on its own.

Notices(ICharacter, ICharacter, out bool)

Whether a wild animal's search should pick other: a predator what it may hunt, a territorial animal what it charges, prey what it flees from. decided is false when the searcher is not wildlife, and the faction rules answer instead. Pure, so it is tested on its own.

public static bool Notices(ICharacter searcher, ICharacter other, out bool decided)

Remarks

A predator hunts everything but its pack. Prey runs from everything but other prey and territorial animals (a boar grazing beside deer does not scatter them). A territorial animal ignores prey and its own kind and charges anything else that comes inside its (short) detection radius.
