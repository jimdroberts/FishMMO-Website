Ambient Life module

Short description: The world's ambient life and site effects, all client presentation: the land's birds, bats and small animals, sea life and what floats on the sea, motes in the air, falling leaves, and the life of the world's sites (fire embers and flames, lighthouse beams, cave drips and water life, sea hall vents, rockfalls).
Module id ambient-life (AmbientLifeModule)
Assemblies FishMMO.AmbientLife, .Client, .Editor, .Tests
Depends on World Simulation (the weather, sky and biomes it draws with); its tests also reference Water
Depended on by None
Remove together with Nothing

Core seams it uses

Every system starts itself in play mode: it hooks the render pipeline after the first scene loads and draws round each camera from the scene's baked data and the shared clock. Nothing is simulated on the server, and nothing is sent.

Seam Where What the module does
Ground cover GroundCoverQuery (core client) Reads the grass cover, so it does not need the Terrain module.
Test bed switches RenderSwitches (core client) ambientLife, ambientLifeMax, ambientHighlight.
Perf report RenderReports (core client) The "ambient life" line in the scene perf probe.
Weather overlay WeatherOverlays (World Simulation) Registers the air motes, drawn with the weather from the weather being shown.
Render profile WeatherRenderAssets.RegisterModuleProfile (World Simulation editor) Fills its own profile whenever the weather's is filled.
Module list [assembly: GameModule] AmbientLifeModule ("ambient-life").

What stays elsewhere

  • In the core: the site data it draws from (the scenes' emitters, cave life spots, sea hall vents and cave water, baked by the world generator and kept in the scenes), and the "Falling Leaves" option (ClientVisualSettings), which does nothing without the module.
  • In World Simulation: whether a fire burns (SiteFireState; the weather decides it and the site lights read it), the cave skylights (CaveSkylights), and the wetland measure (TerrainProcess.Wetland), which moss reads too.

Removing it

Delete Assets/FishMMO/Modules/AmbientLife (to check it, follow the gate). The world then has no animals, sea life, motes, leaves or site effects; nothing else changes.
