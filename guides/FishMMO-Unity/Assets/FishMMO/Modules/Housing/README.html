Core seams it uses

Seam What the module does

SceneServer_Systems label HousingSystem.asset is adopted by the scene server.
ClientWorldGUI_Panels label UIHousingPlot and UIHousingVault prefabs are placed into the world GUI.
[assembly: OperatorCommandProvider] HousingOperatorCommands adds /gm plot and /admin plot (hosted by the Staff module).
BankWindowLinks (core client) "House vault" in the bank window, shown only while the vault holds something.
[assembly: GameModule] HousingModule ("housing").

What stays in the core

The plots are part of the world: the world generator lays them into the scenes, and the scenes keep them whether or not housing is installed. So the core keeps PlotFoundation (and its visual and mesh), IPlotFoundation, the plot's state, identity, owner, access and permission types, ScenePlotManifest, the plot tools, and the ECA actions and the Plot Interact trigger on every deed post.

Removing it

Delete Assets/FishMMO/Modules/Housing (to check it, follow the gate). A deed post then does nothing when used. The plot, structure, access and vault tables and their services stay in place, unused.
