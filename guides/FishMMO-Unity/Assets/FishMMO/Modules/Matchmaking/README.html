Core seams it uses

Seam What the module does

ClientWorldGUI_Panels label The arena board, HUD, results and scoreboard and the dungeon finder are placed into the world GUI.
[assembly: HarmRelation] ArenaHarmRelation: inside a live match the teams decide who may harm whom.
CharacterSystem.OnSpawnCharacter / OnDespawnCharacter / OnDisconnect Arena seating and leaving; the group finder drops a disconnected player.

[assembly: GameModule] MatchmakingModule ("matchmaking").

What stays in the core

The world and its content: dungeon and arena templates, dungeon difficulties (instancing uses them), the dungeon entrances, arena boards and arena objectives placed by the world generator, their interfaces and ECA actions and the two messages those actions send (DungeonFinderBroadcast, ArenaBoardBroadcast), ArenaRespawnBroadcast (the death dialog), ArenaEventKind / ArenaEventData (arena templates' triggers), ArenaTeamRegistry (Factions and arena chat read it), GroupFinderRules and its state and refusal enums, the arena spectator camera, and the ArenaStandings seam.

Removing it

Delete Assets/FishMMO/Modules/Matchmaking (to check it, follow the gate). The dungeon entrances and arena boards then open nothing. The group finder, dungeon instance, arena rating and match tables stay in place, unused.
