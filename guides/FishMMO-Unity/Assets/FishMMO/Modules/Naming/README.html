Dashboard DashboardFullPages The Name Generation page, in the Naming group.
Module list [assembly: GameModule] NamingModule ("naming").

What stays in the core

The naming data on the templates other systems load: RaceNamingData and its serializable types on every race, SerializableBiomePhonology on every biome (World Simulation's biome templates), the shared vocabulary (POIType, CityType, ItemType, TitleRegister, RacePhonology, BiomePhonology), StableHash and GeneratorUtility. And the SceneObjectNamer on the interactables and NPCs, with its SceneObjectNamingSettings: its seed, gender and place travel in its spawn payload whether or not the module is installed.

Removing it

Delete Assets/FishMMO/Modules/Naming and Assets/FishMMO/Modules/WorldDesign (to check it, follow the gate). Every scene object then keeps its authored name.
